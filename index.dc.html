Hardware Music Education

Structured guides for learning synthesis, sampling, beat-making and live performance on Behringer, Roland, Elektron and Novation hardware. Each guide is eight weeks of lessons with interactive demos and persistent progress tracking.

TD-3 & RD-6 Guide

Beginner Hardware Setup & Performance. Learn synthesis, sequencing and live performance on Behringer's acid bassline and drum synthesizers. Eight weeks covering sound design, pattern programming, sync and hardware integration.

Behringer TD-3-MO & RD-6-BK

Sample & Circuit Guide

Groovebox / Sampler Curriculum. Learn sampling, slicing, sequencing and arrangement on hardware or in the browser. Hands-on with a seven-sample groovebox, covering import, resampling, live performance and WAV export.

Circuit Tracks / Rhythm, Model:Samples, SP-404MKII

DDJ-FLX4 Guide

DJ & Beat Production. Beatmatching, mixing, sampling and production on a modern four-channel controller. Eight weeks progressing from basic mixing through advanced beat production and live sampling techniques.

Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 Controller

MPK Mini MK4 Guide

Production & Performance Keyboard. Learn beat production, chord progressions, arpeggiators and live arrangement on a compact keyboard. Covers synth basics, pattern sequencing and multi-track song composition.

Akai MPK Mini MK4 Controller

TR-06 Guide

Pattern Editor & Beat Design. Design drum patterns and sounds on Roland's classic rhythm composer. Learn 16-step programming, swing, timing and the relationship between sound selection and pattern composition.

Roland TR-06 Rhythm Composer
