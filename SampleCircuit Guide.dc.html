Sample & Circuit Guide

Groovebox / Sampler Curriculum

An eight-week, computer-light course for Novation Circuit Tracks / Rhythm, Elektron Model:Samples, Roland SP-404MKII, or the browser sampler below. Treat the sampler as a band in a box: slice, sequence, resample, arrange and perform.

By Week 8 you will be able to

  • Import, organise and safely gain-stage samples.
  • Auto-slice and manually refine a break.
  • Record with or without quantisation and preserve human feel.
  • Chain eight or more patterns into a complete arrangement.
  • Resample internal audio and export a stereo WAV.
  • Perform a three-track mini-set with live mutes and FX moves.
