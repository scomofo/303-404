DJ-404 Performance Guide

Pioneer DJ DDJ-FLX4

Course Overview

  • Target Audience: Absolute Beginners
  • Duration: 8 weeks, five applied sessions per week
  • Equipment Needed:
    • Pioneer DJ DDJ-FLX4 controller
    • USB-C cable (included)
    • Laptop or computer with Serato DJ Lite (bundled, free) or rekordbox installed
    • Headphones (1/4" or 1/8" stereo jack)
    • Powered speakers, monitors, or a DJ mixer/amp for the Master Out
    • Internet connection for initial software activation

The mixer, beatmatching, cue, loop, FX and recording labs use two original synthesized reference loops—not licensed music—so you can practise mechanics before importing your own tracks.

DJing is storytelling. Every week ends with a recorded mini-mix, and Week 8 produces a polished 30-minute set recorded in rekordbox or Serato. The browser recorder is for short drills; it is deliberately capped to avoid allocating a 30-minute PCM file in a browser tab.
