Course Overview

  • Target Audience: Absolute Beginners
  • Duration: 7 weeks of hands-on lessons (2 hours / week), then a self-paced 6-week practice plan
  • Equipment Needed:
    • Pioneer DJ DDJ-FLX4 controller
    • USB-C cable (included)
    • Laptop or computer with Serato DJ Lite (bundled, free) or rekordbox installed
    • Headphones (1/4" or 1/8" stereo jack)
    • Powered speakers, monitors, or a DJ mixer/amp for the Master Out
    • Internet connection for initial software activation

The mixer/beatmatching/hot-cue/loop/FX practice widgets in this course run on two synthesized reference loops (a simple kick + hi-hat pattern), not real music — so you can practice the mechanics of mixing without needing licensed audio. Everything you learn transfers directly to mixing real tracks.

Weeks 1–7 teach the controller. The Practice Plan that follows is a six-week, self-paced curriculum of watching, drilling and recording — built around free YouTube tutorials, with a milestone closing out each week — that takes you from working the controls to a set you'd send to a booker.
