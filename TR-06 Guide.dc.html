TR-06 Guide

Roland Boutique Drumatix — drum companion to the TD-3

Five weeks on the Roland TR-06, the Boutique recreation of the TR-606. It is the drum path next to the TD-3-MO — not the analog RD-6 clone. Write a 16, use the sequencer the 606 never had, open the Inst locker, clock a 303 from the trigger row, then sit both machines in the same headphones.

Course Overview

  • Target Audience: Owners of a Roland TR-06 (Boutique), ideally with a TD-3 / TB-03 / TB-303
  • Duration: 5 Weeks (2–3 Hours / Week)
  • Equipment Needed:
    • Roland TR-06 + DK-01 dock (optional tilt)
    • Four AA batteries or a data-capable micro-USB cable
    • Headphones (3.5 mm)
    • Mono 3.5 mm TS cables for trigger I/O
    • Behringer TD-3-MO or Roland TB-03 for Weeks 4–5
